# Example requests

Replace bracketed text with your question or attach the relevant material. A request does not give intel access to data that your host cannot retrieve.

## Research a company or market

> Use intel to assess [company or market] as of today. Define the market boundary, evaluate industry economics and competitive advantages, identify the strongest countercase, and recommend which opportunity deserves further investigation. Cite pivotal facts and distinguish current evidence from historical examples.

## Challenge a thesis

> Use intel-red-team to challenge this thesis: [thesis]. Inspect the evidence, build the strongest credible rival explanation, and identify the assumptions that could reverse the recommendation. Keep any conclusions that survive the challenge.

## Diagnose a business change

> Use intel-diagnose to explain this change in [metric]. Build a useful issue tree, separate the accounting identity from causal explanations, and identify the next discriminating tests. Do not assume the first plausible explanation is the cause.

## Choose a research-report topic

> Use intel-research to compare audience interest in [topics] among [audience]. Separate measured engagement from editorial judgment, account for different platforms and observation windows, and recommend a practical validation test where the evidence is weak.

## Plan a strategic move

> Use intel to evaluate [proposed decision]. Compare realistic alternatives, assess stakeholder incentives and likely competitor responses, run a pre-mortem, and recommend an action with clear review triggers.

## Explore a new market

> Use intel-innovate to examine [idea]. Identify buyer problems and noncustomers, develop an eliminate-reduce-raise-create proposal, test utility, pricing, costs, and adoption, and propose the smallest experiment that could disprove demand.

## Forecast an outcome

> Use intel-foresight to assess whether [measurable event] will occur by [date]. Define the resolution rule, examine relevant base rates and current evidence, state the basis for any probability, and specify evidence that would trigger an update.

## Prepare a decision memo

> Use intel-brief to turn the attached research into a memo for [audience]. Lead with the decision, cite the pivotal evidence, identify the strongest alternative and material gaps, and give the next action and what would change the recommendation.
